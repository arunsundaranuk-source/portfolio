About me

Engineering products that can be made

From injection molds for automotive trims to Philips patient monitoring and ASML semiconductor systems, I bring tooling know-how, design controls and clear documentation to every project.

I'm a Lead Mechanical Design Engineer with more than ten years across regulated medical devices, semiconductor inspection equipment, IoT and connected products, consumer electronics and high-volume manufacturing.

I trained in tool and die engineering and spent my first four years designing injection molds for automotive and handheld products. At Cyient I delivered ISO 13485 medical-device work for Philips Healthcare. At Zealogics I led electro-mechanical design and documentation for ASML programmes including eScan 1100, eScan 2200 and EXE5000 / NXE 5000, working in Siemens NX and Teamcenter.

I'm currently completing a B.Tech in Engineering Technology at BITS Pilani alongside work.
